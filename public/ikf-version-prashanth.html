What we do

Three pillars. One mission.

01

Player Pathway

From district scout to academy trial. We replace guesswork with a 360° view of every child — football, mind, body and academics.

02

Parent Empowerment

Most parents don't fail their kids — the system fails them. We teach what academies actually look for, in their language.

03

Coach Network

India's coaches are isolated. We connect them, train them, and let them refer talent across states and leagues.

Safeguarding

Every child has the right to feel safe.

See something? Say something — anonymously.
Report abuse, fake agents, predatory academies, financial pressure, or anything that doesn't feel right. IKF safeguarding lead responds within 24 hrs.

⚠️ Warning signs

Upfront "trial fees", guaranteed selection promises, contracts in English-only, isolation from family.

📜 Your rights

No legitimate academy charges for trials. You can withdraw your child at any time. POCSO protections apply.
